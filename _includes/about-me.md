I am an undergraduate student in Applied Physics at the School of Physics, Xi’an Jiaotong University. My research interests span quantum computing and quantum information, including quantum algorithms for solving partial differential equations (PDEs), the design of novel quantum teleportation protocols, and quantum error correction.

Under the supervision of [Professor Jibo He](https://people.ucas.ac.cn/~he), I am currently exploring quantum search methods for space-based gravitational-wave detection.

More recently, I have become interested in machine learning for physics and condensed matter theory, particularly the use of physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) for scientific computing.
