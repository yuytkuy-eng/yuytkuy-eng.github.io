A spectral gap for the spin-2 square-lattice AKLT model

UNSOLVED
Fields
Quantum lattice systems
Topics
AKLTspectral gapPEPSfrustration freetwo dimensions

Problem

Definitions

Put a spin-2 degree of freedom at each vertex of an L×L square torus and set

HL=∑⟨x,y⟩Pxy(4),H_L = \sum_{⟨x,y⟩} P^{(4)}_{xy},HL​=⟨x,y⟩∑​Pxy(4)​,

where P^{(4)}_{xy} projects the two neighboring spins onto total spin 4.

Assumptions

Spin two, even square tori, undecorated AKLT projector Hamiltonian.

Claim

Prove that the AKLT ground-state sector is separated from the rest of the spectrum by a constant Δ>0 independent of L.

[1]
