The order-one term in the strong-coupling Levy–Lieb expansion

UNSOLVED
Fields
Density functional theory
Topics
strictly correlated electronsstrong couplingLevy-Lieb functionalzero-point energyoptimal transport

Problem

Assumptions

Fixed smooth density and explicitly regular strictly correlated minimizers; interaction strength tends to infinity at unchanged density.

Claim

Fix a smooth strictly positive density ρ on a bounded three-dimensional domain, with ∫ρ=N, for which the strictly correlated electron minimizer is sufficiently regular and nondegenerate. For

Fλ[ρ]=infΨ→ρ⟨Ψ,(T+λVee)Ψ⟩,F_λ[ρ]=inf_{Ψ→ρ}⟨Ψ,(T+λV_ee)Ψ⟩,Fλ​[ρ]=infΨ→ρ​⟨Ψ,(T+λVe​e)Ψ⟩,

prove an expansion

Fλ[ρ]=λVSCE[ρ]+λ1/2FZPE[ρ]+F2[ρ]+o(1)F_λ[ρ]=λV_SCE[ρ]+λ^{1/2}F_ZPE[ρ]+F_2[ρ]+o(1)Fλ​[ρ]=λVS​CE[ρ]+λ1/2FZ​PE[ρ]+F2​[ρ]+o(1)

as λ→∞, and give an intrinsic formula for F_2[ρ], including exchange symmetry and tunneling between optimal-transport branches.
