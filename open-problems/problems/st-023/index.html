The Wu logarithmic correction for the three-dimensional hard-sphere Bose gas

UNSOLVED
Fields
Quantum gases
Topics
dilute Bose gashard spheresground-state energyWu correction

Problem

Definitions

Let e(ρ) be the thermodynamic ground-state energy density of three-dimensional hard spheres of scattering length a.

Assumptions

Hard spheres with fixed scattering length; the thermodynamic limit precedes the dilute limit.

Claim

Prove, as ρa³→0,

e(ρ)=4πaρ²[1 + 128/(15√π)·√(ρa³) + 8(4π/3−√3) ρa³ log(ρa³) + o(ρa³ log(ρa³))].
