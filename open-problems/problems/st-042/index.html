Infinitely many real magic angles in the chiral model of twisted bilayer graphene

UNSOLVED

Problem

Definitions

Let D(α) be the chiral Bistritzer–MacDonald Hamiltonian block with the standard Tarnopolsky–Kruchkov–Vishwanath tunnelling potential, so that a parameter α is magic precisely when D(α) exhibits a flat band at zero energy.

Assumptions

Real positive coupling and the fixed unperturbed potential; specify multiplicity and ordering of magic parameters.

Claim

Prove that the set of real magic parameters α₁<α₂<... is infinite, and prove the numerically observed asymptotic quantization

αk+1−αk→3/2ask→∞.α_{k+1} − α_k → 3/2 as k→∞.αk+1​−αk​→3/2ask→∞.

A secondary target is simplicity: prove that for the TKV potential every real magic parameter carries a flat band of minimal multiplicity.
