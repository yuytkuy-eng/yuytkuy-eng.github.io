Fractional charge and exchange phase of pinned Laughlin quasiholes

UNSOLVED
Fields
Fractional quantum Hall states
Topics
Laughlin quasiholefractional chargeBerry phaseanyonsplasma screening

Problem

Definitions

Add two well-separated localized pinning potentials to the bosonic ν=1/2 Laughlin pseudopotential Hamiltonian on a disk or sphere and choose a precise adiabatic path exchanging their centers.

Assumptions

Explicit pinning potentials and exchange path; quasihole separations exceed magnetic length but remain small compared with sample size; spectral isolation is part of the proof.

Claim

Prove, uniformly as particle number and separation grow, that each quasihole carries missing particle number 1/2 and that the exchange Berry phase, after subtraction of the Aharonov–Bohm contribution, converges to π/2 modulo 2π.
