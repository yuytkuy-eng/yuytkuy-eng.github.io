The gap is the first distinct energy above the ground-state sector. Thermodynamic limits use the stated boundary and state-selection conventions.

Use the fermionic V1V_1V1​ pseudopotential on a torus and bosonic V0V_0V0​ on a sphere, with fixed filling and energy normalization.

Assumptions

Particle number tends to infinity; torus aspect ratio stays in a compact nondegenerate range.

Claim

For each of these two models, prove a particle-number-independent lower bound on the first positive eigenvalue.
