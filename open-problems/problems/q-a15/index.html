Quantum complexity of specified lattice regimes

UNSOLVED

complexity

Problem

Definitions

Use uniform polynomial-size circuits and input bit length as the complexity parameter. BQP has no witness; QMA has a polynomial-qubit witness; QCMA has a polynomial-bit witness; QMA(2) has two unentangled quantum witnesses. Standard bounded-error verification has completeness at least 2/32/32/3 and soundness at most 1/31/31/3.

LWE samples are (a,⟨a,s⟩+e mod q)(a,\langle a,s\rangle+e\bmod q)(a,⟨a,s⟩+emodq); lattice approximation tasks include SVP and CVP.

Assumptions

Specify dimension, modulus, error law, sample number, approximation factor and classical or quantum input access.

Claim

Determine the quantum running-time frontier for search and decision LWE with explicitly specified dimension nnn, modulus qqq, error distribution and sample access, and for SVP/CVP with stated approximation factor. A focal LWE regime has polynomial modulus and inverse-polynomial relative Gaussian error; hardness must be assessed for each concrete parameter family.
