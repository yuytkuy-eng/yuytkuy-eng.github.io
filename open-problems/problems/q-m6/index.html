Assumptions

A specified family with a uniform unperturbed gap and ground-state structure beyond existing product, frustration-free or local-order criteria.

Claim

Extend verifiable bulk-gap stability criteria to a specified interacting lattice family outside current theorems. Fix the unperturbed gap, ground-space assumptions and interaction-decay norm, and prove a system-size-independent perturbation radius preserving a positive bulk gap.
