Progress

  • Deng, Hani, and Ma prove convergence throughout the lifespan of the classical Boltzmann solution for their data class, extending Lanford's short-time interval. The work was accepted by Annals of Mathematics on 31 October 2025; it covers Euclidean dimensions at least two and periodic dimensions two and three.
