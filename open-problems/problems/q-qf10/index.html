Fix Wightman fields, a Haag–Kastler local net or Osterwalder–Schrader Schwinger functions, including covariance, locality, vacuum and positive-energy axioms. A Hamiltonian mass gap means spec⁡H∩(0,Δ)=∅\operatorname{spec}H\cap(0,\Delta)=\varnothingspecH∩(0,Δ)=∅ for some Δ>0\Delta>0Δ>0.

A locally covariant theory is a functor from globally hyperbolic spacetimes to observable algebras, with admissible Hadamard states.

Assumptions

Fixed field, dimension, curved-spacetime class and preparation or boundary data; seek an actual interacting construction.

Claim

For a specified interacting field model and class of globally hyperbolic curved spacetimes, construct local observables and admissible states beyond formal perturbation theory; state any extra boundary, symmetry or preparation data used to select a state.
