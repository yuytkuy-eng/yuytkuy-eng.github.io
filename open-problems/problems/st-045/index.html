Kohn–Luttinger superconductivity in the dilute repulsive Fermi gas

Problem

Definitions

Consider spin-1/2 fermions in the three-dimensional continuum at density ρ, interacting through a repulsive short-range pair potential of positive scattering length a. Let γ^{(2)} be the two-particle reduced density matrix of a thermodynamic ground state.

Assumptions

The thermodynamic limit precedes dilution; sufficiently small positive gas parameter and a fixed repulsive potential class.

Claim

Decide whether, for all sufficiently small ρa³, the ground state exhibits pairing off-diagonal long-range order,
