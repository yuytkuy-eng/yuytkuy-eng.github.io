Existence or triviality of continuum QED₄

UNSOLVED

Problem

Definitions

Fix Wightman fields, a Haag–Kastler local net or Osterwalder–Schrader Schwinger functions, including covariance, locality, vacuum and positive-energy axioms. A Hamiltonian mass gap means spec⁡H∩(0,Δ)=∅\operatorname{spec}H\cap(0,\Delta)=\varnothingspecH∩(0,Δ)=∅ for some Δ>0\Delta>0Δ>0.

QED triviality means renormalized charge tends to zero as the ultraviolet cutoff is removed; specify gauge-invariant observables and infrared charged sectors.

Assumptions

Fixed physical renormalization conditions and axioms in four-dimensional QED.

Claim

Either construct nontrivial four-dimensional continuum QED satisfying specified relativistic QFT axioms, or prove that removing the ultraviolet cutoff forces its renormalized interaction to vanish; control the charge and infrared sectors in either formulation.
