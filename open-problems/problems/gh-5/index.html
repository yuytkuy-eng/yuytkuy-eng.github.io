Definitions

Colliding masses merge with m=∑mim=\sum m_im=∑mi​ and v=∑mivi/mv=\sum m_iv_i/mv=∑mi​vi​/m. Continuum motion obeys pressureless mass and momentum conservation, permanent sticking and nonincreasing kinetic energy.

Assumptions

Dimension greater than one; specify potential or general velocities, admissible data and collision selection.

Claim

Find an extended configuration space and least-action principle producing sticking motion and shock surfaces, with existence, selection of nonunique solutions and a continuum limit.
