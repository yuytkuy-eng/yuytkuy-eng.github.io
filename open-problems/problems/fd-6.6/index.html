Realizing affine foliations as momentum images

UNSOLVED
Fields
Hamiltonian MechanicsIntegrable SystemsSymplectic Geometry
Topics
affine foliationmomentum imagerealization

Problem

Definitions

The base BBB has an integral affine foliation with transverse Casimir directions. In the symplectic case, seek a Lagrangian torus fibration M2n→BnM^{2n}\to B^nM2n→Bn.

Assumptions

Fix compact fibers, properness, allowed Chern classes and base topology; treat boundary and singular cases separately.

Claim

Give necessary and sufficient realizability conditions and constructions, including symplectic realizations of specified closed bases.
