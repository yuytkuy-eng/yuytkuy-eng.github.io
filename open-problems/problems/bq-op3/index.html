Orbit behavior in irrational brick tilings

UNSOLVED
Fields
Classical MechanicsBilliards and Symplectic Geometry
Topics
brick tilingirrational parametertiling billiard

Problem

Definitions

A periodic polygonal tiling has finitely many prototypes modulo a rank-two translation lattice. Unit-speed rays cross edges with the tangential velocity reversed and the normal component into the next tile retained, corresponding to refractive index −1-1−1. Exclude vertex collisions; typicality uses position times direction Lebesgue measure.

Alternate rows of unit squares are shifted horizontally by θ∈(0,1)\theta\in(0,1)θ∈(0,1).

Assumptions

Irrational θ\thetaθ and nonsingular position–direction typical initial data.

Claim

Determine the measure of periodic initial data, unbounded escape behavior and orbit closures, and whether a parameter-typical classification exists.
