Definitions

Fix Wightman fields, a Haag–Kastler local net or Osterwalder–Schrader Schwinger functions, including covariance, locality, vacuum and positive-energy axioms. A Hamiltonian mass gap means spec⁡H∩(0,Δ)=∅\operatorname{spec}H\cap(0,\Delta)=\varnothingspecH∩(0,Δ)=∅ for some Δ>0\Delta>0Δ>0.

Gauss constraints, region observable algebras, centers and accessible boundary modes determine the subsystem convention.

Assumptions

Specified gauge-invariant operations, boundary charges, regulator and continuum limit.

Claim

For specified gauge-invariant operations and boundary access, characterize distillable resources of spatial subsystems and prove when their continuum predictions are regulator independent; compare different access models without assuming a unique factorization.
