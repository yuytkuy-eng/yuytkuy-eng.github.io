Multiple unentangled quantum proofs

UNSOLVED
Fields
Quantum ComputingComputational Complexity
Topics
complexity

Problem

Definitions

Use uniform polynomial-size circuits and input bit length as the complexity parameter. BQP has no witness; QMA has a polynomial-qubit witness; QCMA has a polynomial-bit witness; QMA(2) has two unentangled quantum witnesses. Standard bounded-error verification has completeness at least 2/32/32/3 and soundness at most 1/31/31/3.

In QMA(2), witnesses are ρA⊗σB\rho_A\otimes\sigma_BρA​⊗σB​; the verifier may jointly operate on both registers.

Assumptions

Polynomial witness sizes; no oracle, extra interaction or postselection.

Claim

Determine whether QMA(2)=QMA\mathsf{QMA}(2)=\mathsf{QMA}QMA(2)=QMA or QMA⊊QMA(2)\mathsf{QMA}\subsetneq\mathsf{QMA}(2)QMA⊊QMA(2).

Progress

  • Logarithmically many shared EPR pairs do not increase QMA(2) in the specified model; not QMA(2)=QMA.[4]
  • Quantum-oracle QMA(2)/QMA separation and an unrelativized no-disentanglers theorem at constant errors with epsilon + delta < 1.[3]
  • A quantum oracle separation between QMA(2) and QMA (2026)[3]
