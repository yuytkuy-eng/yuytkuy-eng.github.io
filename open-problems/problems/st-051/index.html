A uniform gap above the bosonic Moore–Read state

UNSOLVED
Fields
Fractional quantum Hall states
Topics
Moore-Read statePfaffianparent Hamiltonianspectral gapnon-Abelian topological order

Problem

Definitions

For even N bosons in the lowest Landau level on the sphere at flux N_φ=N-2, let H_N be the positive three-body contact pseudopotential whose unique zero mode is the bosonic Moore–Read state.

Assumptions

Fixed particle-number-independent pseudopotential normalization for the Moore–Read zero-mode parent.

Claim

Prove that there is Δ>0, independent of N, such that the smallest positive eigenvalue of H_N is at least Δ.
