Problem

Definitions

In a planar billiard table Ω⊂R2\Omega\subset\mathbb R^2Ω⊂R2, a unit-speed particle moves along straight lines and reflects by v↦v−2⟨v,n⟩nv\mapsto v-2\langle v,n\rangle nv↦v−2⟨v,n⟩n. The collision map TTT acts on Φ=∂Ω×(−π/2,π/2)\Phi=\partial\Omega\times(-\pi/2,\pi/2)Φ=∂Ω×(−π/2,π/2), with boundary arclength sss and angle φ\varphiφ to the inward normal. Its invariant probability measure is dμ=Z−1cos⁡φ ds dφ.d\mu=Z^{-1}\cos\varphi\,ds\,d\varphi.dμ=Z−1cosφdsdφ. Vertex hits and grazing collisions are excluded from the regular phase space.

Let Pk={z∈Φ:Tkz=z}P_k=\{z\in\Phi:T^kz=z\}Pk​={z∈Φ:Tkz=z}, with all iterates regular, and P=⋃k≥1PkP=\bigcup_{k\ge1}P_kP=⋃k≥1​Pk​.

Assumptions

The table is an arbitrary bounded smooth planar domain; convexity is not assumed.

Claim

Is μ(Pk)=0\mu(P_k)=0μ(Pk​)=0 for every k≥1k\ge1k≥1, equivalently μ(P)=0\mu(P)=0μ(P)=0?

Source

Ivrii's 1980 spectral asymptotics theorem uses the zero-measure hypothesis for periodic trajectories.

Progress

  • The conjecture is proved for periods two and three, and for period four in the plane. The statement for all periods remains open.
