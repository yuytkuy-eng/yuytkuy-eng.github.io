A three-dimensional finite-range quasicrystalline Gibbs phase

UNSOLVED
Fields
Equilibrium statistical mechanics and quasicrystals
Topics
quasicrystalaperiodic orderGibbs statefinite-range interactionlattice gas

Problem

Assumptions

A three-dimensional finite-alphabet finite-range model with explicit pure-state-selecting boundary conditions.

Claim

Construct an explicit finite alphabet A and a translation-invariant finite-range interaction Φ on A^{Z³} such that

(i) Φ has a unique translation-invariant ground-state measure, and that measure is supported on aperiodic configurations; and (ii) for every sufficiently large inverse temperature β, the model has a nonperiodic extremal Gibbs state μ_β that is a small local perturbation of the ground-state measure—for example, with a proved sea-island bound on defects.

The alphabet, interaction table, range, and quantitative stability notion must all be stated explicitly.
