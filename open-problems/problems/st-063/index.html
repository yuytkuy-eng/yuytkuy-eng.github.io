A uniform gap for the bosonic Z3 Read–Rezayi parent Hamiltonian

UNSOLVED

Topics

Problem

Definitions

For N divisible by three bosons in the lowest Landau level on the sphere at flux N_φ=2N/3-2, let H_N be the positive four-body contact pseudopotential whose unique densest zero mode is the k=3 Read–Rezayi state.

Assumptions

A particle-number-independent gap above the entire zero-mode sector.

Claim

Prove that the smallest positive eigenvalue of H_N is bounded below by a constant Δ>0 independent of N.
