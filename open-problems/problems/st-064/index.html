Interacting v-representability of a regular continuum density class

UNSOLVED
Fields
Density functional theory
Topics
interacting v-representabilityLieb functionalCoulomb systemsinverse problemdensity class

Problem

Assumptions

Three-dimensional Coulomb fermions, potentials in L3/2+L∞L^{3/2}+L^\inftyL3/2+L∞ and an explicit regular density class.

Claim

Fix N≥2 and let D_reg be the class of densities ρ∈C^∞(R^3) satisfying ρ>0, ∫ρ=N, √ρ∈H^1(R^3), and two-sided exponential decay with stated constants. Characterize the subset of D_reg that is interacting v-representable for the Coulomb interaction: for which ρ does there exist v∈L^{3/2}(R^3)+L^∞(R^3), unique modulo a constant when the ground state is nondegenerate, whose N-electron ground-state density is ρ? In particular, prove that all densities in one explicit nonempty open subclass are representable, or give a regular density with a rigorous obstruction.
