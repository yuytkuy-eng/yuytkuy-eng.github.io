Problem

Definitions

Use uniform polynomial-size circuits and input bit length as the complexity parameter. BQP has no witness; QMA has a polynomial-qubit witness; QCMA has a polynomial-bit witness; QMA(2) has two unentangled quantum witnesses. Standard bounded-error verification has completeness at least 2/32/32/3 and soundness at most 1/31/31/3.

H=∑j=1mPjH=\sum_{j=1}^mP_jH=∑j=1m​Pj​ has mutually commuting kkk-local orthogonal projectors on fixed-dimensional sites, with finite rational or algebraic descriptions.

Assumptions

Fixed k,dk,dk,d, polynomial input size and an inverse-polynomial energy promise gap; arbitrary interaction graphs.

Claim

For every fixed locality kkk and local dimension ddd, is the commuting-projector local-Hamiltonian promise problem in NP\mathsf{NP}NP? The input has polynomially many commuting kkk-local projectors with finite rational/algebraic descriptions and inverse-polynomially separated ground-energy thresholds; no interaction geometry is assumed.
