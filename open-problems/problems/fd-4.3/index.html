Conic rigidity of projective tangent involutions

UNSOLVED
Fields
Hamiltonian MechanicsIntegrable SystemsSymplectic Geometry
Topics
projective billiardconic pencilinvolution

Problem

Definitions

Let γ⊂RP2\gamma\subset\mathbb{RP}^2γ⊂RP2 be a smooth strictly convex closed curve with a collar foliated by closed convex curves, including γ\gammaγ. On each tangent line ℓ\ellℓ, pairing the two intersections with each leaf defines a nontrivial local involution σℓ\sigma_\ellσℓ​ fixing the tangency point.

Assumptions

Every σℓ\sigma_\ellσℓ​ is a projective transformation of ℓ≃RP1\ell\simeq\mathbb{RP}^1ℓ≃RP1, with a complete collar of closed leaves.

Claim

Must the boundary and all foliation leaves belong to one pencil of conics?
