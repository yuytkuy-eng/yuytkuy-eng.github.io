Global action-angle obstructions for regular Poisson systems

SOLVED
Fields
Hamiltonian MechanicsIntegrable SystemsSymplectic Geometry
Topics
regular Poissonglobal action-anglemonodromy

Problem

Definitions

On an mmm-manifold with a Poisson tensor of generic rank 2r2r2r, an integrable map F=(f1,…,fm−r)F=(f_1,\ldots,f_{m-r})F=(f1​,…,fm−r​) has independent differentials and pairwise Poisson-commuting components. Its compact joint Hamiltonian leaves are rrr-tori. At regular points, action-angle coordinates give Π=∑i=1r∂θi∧∂Ii\Pi=\sum_{i=1}^r\partial_{\theta_i}\wedge\partial_{I_i}Π=∑i=1r​∂θi​​∧∂Ii​​, with transverse Casimir coordinates.

The period lattice Λ→B\Lambda\to BΛ→B determines a torus-bundle Chern class in H2(B,Λ)H^2(B,\Lambda)H2(B,Λ); action, angle and transverse foliations have compatibility obstructions.

Assumptions

A regular Poisson structure and a surjective integral-map submersion with compact connected fibers.

Claim

Compute all obstructions to global action and angle variables and their joint compatibility, with a necessary and sufficient existence theorem.
