Problem

Definitions

In fixed data and solution spaces X,YX,YX,Y, nonlinear asymptotic stability means that every sufficiently small constraint-compatible perturbation has a complete future exterior and converges, modulo gauge, to a nearby stationary endstate. Unless uniformity is explicitly required, the smallness threshold and estimates may depend on the fixed background.

In dimension D≥5D\ge5D≥5, the vacuum family is asymptotically flat Myers–Perry with rotation parameters aia_iai​.

Assumptions

Fixed dimension and horizon topology, initially small angular momenta and smooth weighted perturbations.

Claim

Determine PDE linear and nonlinear stability or instability ranges and endstates, including dimension and ultraspinning effects.
