Assumptions

Two-dimensional finite-range noncommuting models under verifiable assumptions beyond high temperature, with quasilocal samplers and implementation cost.

Claim

For two-dimensional finite-range noncommuting spin Hamiltonians of bounded local strength and a specified reversible local or quasilocal Gibbs sampler, identify verifiable temperature/interaction conditions beyond the established high-temperature regime giving polynomial mixing and preparation to trace-distance error ε\varepsilonε, or prove slow-mixing obstructions.
