A quantitative Hohenberg–Kohn inverse theorem in three dimensions

UNSOLVED

Problem

Definitions

Fix an N-electron Coulomb Hamiltonian with external potential v_0 and a nondegenerate ground state separated by gap g>0. Choose explicit Sobolev-type spaces V/R for potentials modulo constants and D for densities.

Assumptions

A three-dimensional nondegenerate Coulomb ground-state neighborhood with gap at least g>0g>0g>0; choose valid potential and density norms.

Claim

Determine whether there are neighborhoods U of v_0 and constants C,α>0 such that for all v,w∈U with the same uniform gap bound,

∣∣v−w∣∣V/R≤C∣∣ρv−ρw∣∣Dα.||v-w||_{V/R} ≤ C ||ρ_v-ρ_w||_D^α.∣∣v−w∣∣V/R​≤C∣∣ρv​−ρw​∣∣Dα​.

Prove such an estimate for one physically useful pair of spaces, or construct a sequence disproving every estimate of the stated form.
