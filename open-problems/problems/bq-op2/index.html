Typical dynamics of parallelogram tiling billiards

UNSOLVED

Problem

Definitions

A periodic polygonal tiling has finitely many prototypes modulo a rank-two translation lattice. Unit-speed rays cross edges with the tangential velocity reversed and the normal component into the next tile retained, corresponding to refractive index −1-1−1. Exclude vertex collisions; typicality uses position times direction Lebesgue measure.

Tiles have generators (a,0)(a,0)(a,0) and (bsin⁡α,bcos⁡α)(b\sin\alpha,b\cos\alpha)(bsinα,bcosα); modulo scale the parameters are α\alphaα and m=a/bm=a/bm=a/b.

Assumptions

a,b>0a,b>0a,b>0, 0<α<π0<\alpha<\pi0<α<π.

Claim

Classify almost-everywhere orbit closures, periodicity, escape and asymptotic velocity by (α,m)(\alpha,m)(α,m), with exceptional sets.
