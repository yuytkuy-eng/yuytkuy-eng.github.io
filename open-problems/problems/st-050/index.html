120-degree long-range order in the spin-1/2 triangular-lattice Heisenberg antiferromagnet

UNSOLVED
Fields
Quantum magnetism
Topics

Problem

Assumptions

Spin-one-half triangular nearest-neighbor antiferromagnet on compatible periodic tori.

Claim

Let H_L=Σ_{⟨x,y⟩}S_x·S_y on a sequence of periodic triangular tori compatible with the three-sublattice ordering wavevector K. If ψ_L is a ground state, prove that the magnetic structure factor has a macroscopic Bragg component:

lim⁡infL→∞∣ΛL∣−2∑x,y∈ΛLcos⁡(K⋅(x−y))⟨ψL,Sx⋅SyψL⟩>0.\lim inf_{L→∞} |Λ_L|^{-2} \sum_{x,y∈Λ_L} \cos(K·(x-y)) ⟨ψ_L,S_x·S_y ψ_L⟩ > 0.liminfL→∞​∣ΛL​∣−2x,y∈ΛL​∑​cos(K⋅(x−y))⟨ψL​,Sx​⋅Sy​ψL​⟩>0.

An equivalent symmetry-breaking infinite-volume formulation is acceptable.
