A hexatic Gibbs phase for two-dimensional hard disks

UNSOLVED
Fields
Classical statistical mechanics
Topics
hard diskshexatic phaseKTHNYbond-orientational orderGibbs states

Problem

Assumptions

An infinite-volume DLR activity interval, algebraic bond-orientational decay and exponential translational decay without Bragg order.

Claim

For unit hard disks in R^2, prove that there is a nonempty interval of activities or packing fractions admitting an infinite-volume DLR Gibbs state whose sixfold bond-orientational correlation decays algebraically, while translational correlations decay exponentially and no Bragg peak is present. Alternatively, prove that no such hexatic Gibbs phase exists.
