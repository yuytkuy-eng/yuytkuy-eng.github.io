Problem

Definitions

Consider a one-dimensional chain with Hamiltonian

HN=∑i[pi2/2+qi4/4]+∑i(qi+1−qi)2/2,H_N = \sum_i [p_i²/2 + q_i⁴/4] + \sum_i (q_{i+1}−q_i)²/2,HN​=i∑​[pi2​/2+qi4​/4]+i∑​(qi+1​−qi​)2/2,

coupled only at its two endpoints to Langevin heat baths at temperatures T_L≠T_R.

Reservoir temperatures T±δT/2T\pm\delta T/2T±δT/2; linear response δT→0\delta T\to0δT→0 precedes N→∞N\to\inftyN→∞; no stochastic bulk.

Claim

Prove that the stationary energy current J_N satisfies

limN→∞NJN=κ(Tˉ)(TL−TR)lim_{N→∞} N J_N = κ(T̄)(T_L−T_R)limN→∞​NJN​=κ(Tˉ)(TL​−TR​)
