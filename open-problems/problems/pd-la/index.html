Claim

Progress

  • Guillen and Silvestre resolve the spatially homogeneous Coulomb equation. Golding and Henderson's 2026 result treats the inhomogeneous Landau–Fermi–Dirac equation, whose Pauli saturation gives an additional uniform bound. The classical inhomogeneous problem remains open.
