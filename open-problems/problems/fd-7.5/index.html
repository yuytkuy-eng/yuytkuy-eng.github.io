The semitoric inverse spectral conjecture

SOLVED

Problem

Definitions

Let Pj,hP_{j,h}Pj,h​ be pairwise exactly commuting self-adjoint semiclassical pseudodifferential or Berezin–Toeplitz operators with principal symbols pjp_jpj​. The semiclassical parameter approaches zero along a fixed quantization sequence. Write Σh\Sigma_hΣh​ for the joint spectrum. Regular Bohr–Sommerfeld conditions have the form I(E;h)∈2πhZnI(E;h)\in2\pi h\mathbb Z^nI(E;h)∈2πhZn, with fixed Maslov and subprincipal conventions.

A simple semitoric system has dimension four and F=(J,H)F=(J,H)F=(J,H), where JJJ is proper and generates an effective circle action, all singularities are nondegenerate without hyperbolic blocks, and each level of JJJ contains at most one focus-focus point.

Assumptions

A proper simple semitoric system and its full standard semiclassical joint spectrum, with the specified O(h2)O(h^2)O(h2) precision.

Claim

Recover all classical symplectic invariants, including polygon, focus-focus heights, Taylor series and twisting indices, and reconstruct the system's isomorphism class.
