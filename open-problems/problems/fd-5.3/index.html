Smoothness of bi-Lagrangian Grassmannians

CLAIMED
Fields
Hamiltonian MechanicsIntegrable SystemsSymplectic Geometry
Topics
bi-Lagrangian Grassmannianalgebraic variety

Problem

Definitions

For alternating forms A,BA,BA,B on a finite-dimensional real or complex vector space VVV, set r=max⁡λrank⁡(A+λB)r=\max_\lambda\operatorname{rank}(A+\lambda B)r=maxλ​rank(A+λB) and d=dim⁡V−r/2d=\dim V-r/2d=dimV−r/2. The reduced bi-Lagrangian Grassmannian is BLG⁡(A,B)={L∈Gr⁡(d,V):A∣L=B∣L=0}\operatorname{BLG}(A,B)=\{L\in\operatorname{Gr}(d,V):A|_L=B|_L=0\}BLG(A,B)={L∈Gr(d,V):A∣L​=B∣L​=0}. Set Aut⁡(A,B)={g∈GL⁡(V):g∗A=A, g∗B=B}\operatorname{Aut}(A,B)=\{g\in\operatorname{GL}(V):g^*A=A,\ g^*B=B\}Aut(A,B)={g∈GL(V):g∗A=A, g∗B=B}.

Assumptions

Over C\mathbb CC, use the reduced algebraic variety; over R\mathbb RR, distinguish algebraic smoothness from smoothness of the real locus.

Claim

Give a necessary and sufficient Jordan–Kronecker criterion for smoothness, including the proposed case in which all alternating Jordan blocks have dimension two.
