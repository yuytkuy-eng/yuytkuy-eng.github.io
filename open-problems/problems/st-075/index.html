Aging for the Langevin dynamics of the spherical p-spin glass

UNSOLVED

Problem

Definitions

For each N, let H_N be the centered Gaussian field on the sphere S_N={σ∈R^N:|σ|²=N} with covariance

E[HN(σ)HN(τ)]=(N/2)(N−1σ⋅τ)3.E[H_N(σ)H_N(τ)] = (N/2)(N^{-1}σ·τ)^3.E[HN​(σ)HN​(τ)]=(N/2)(N−1σ⋅τ)3.

At temperature T=1/2, let σ_N(t) be the spherical Langevin diffusion with generator

LN=TΔSN−∇SNHN⋅∇SN,L_N = T Δ_{S_N} - ∇_{S_N}H_N · ∇_{S_N},LN​=TΔSN​​−∇SN​​HN​⋅∇SN​​,

started from the uniform probability measure on S_N. Define

C(t2,t1)=limN→∞N−1E[σN(t2)⋅σN(t1)],C(t_2,t_1)=lim_{N→∞} N^{-1} E[σ_N(t_2)·σ_N(t_1)],C(t2​,t1​)=limN→∞​N−1E[σN​(t2​)⋅σN​(t1​)],

where the expectation includes disorder and dynamics and the limit is the Cugliandolo–Kurchan correlation.

Assumptions

The specified stochastic model and parameters, uniform initial state and volume limit before long time; average disorder and dynamics.

Claim

Prove

limt2→∞C(t2,t1)=0foreveryfixedt1,lim_{t_2→∞} C(t_2,t_1)=0 for every fixed t_1,limt2​→∞​C(t2​,t1​)=0foreveryfixedt1​,

while

limt→∞limt1→∞C(t1+t,t1)=q>0.lim_{t→∞} lim_{t_1→∞} C(t_1+t,t_1)=q>0.limt→∞​limt1​→∞​C(t1​+t,t1​)=q>0.

For this normalization the predicted dynamical transition is T_d=√(3/8)>1/2. The two displayed limits therefore state weak ergodicity breaking at one fully fixed low temperature.
