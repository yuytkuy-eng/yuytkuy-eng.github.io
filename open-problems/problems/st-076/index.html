The KPZ fluctuation exponent for one-dimensional ballistic deposition

UNSOLVED
Fields
Stochastic growth and interface dynamics
Topics
KPZ universalityballistic depositioninterface fluctuationsscaling exponents

Problem

Assumptions

The specified ballistic-deposition rule and long-time limit.

Claim

Let independent rate-one Poisson clocks ring at the sites of Z. When the clock at x rings, update the height configuration by

h(x)←max⁡h(x−1),h(x)+1,h(x+1),h(x) ← \max{h(x-1), h(x)+1, h(x+1)},h(x)←maxh(x−1),h(x)+1,h(x+1),

leaving all other heights unchanged. Starting from h≡0, prove

Var⁡[h(0,t)]=t2/3+o(1)ast→∞.\operatorname{Var}[h(0,t)] = t^{2/3+o(1)} as t→∞.Var[h(0,t)]=t2/3+o(1)ast→∞.

Equivalently, prove the KPZ growth exponent β=1/3 for this single, fully specified, non-integrable model.
