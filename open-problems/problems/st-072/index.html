The physical Gell-Mann–Brueckner formula for three-dimensional jellium

Problem

Definitions

Let e_c(r_s) be the exact correlation energy per particle of spin-unpolarized three-dimensional jellium after the thermodynamic limit, defined relative to Hartree–Fock.

Assumptions

The thermodynamic limit precedes rs↓0r_s\downarrow0rs​↓0 at the physical Coulomb coupling.

Claim

Prove the physical high-density expansion

ec(rs)=Alog⁡rs+B+o(1),rs↓0,e_c(r_s)=A \log r_s+B+o(1), r_s↓0,ec​(rs​)=Alogrs​+B+o(1),rs​↓0,

with A and B equal to the Gell-Mann–Brueckner constants, and justify the order of the thermodynamic and high-density limits.
