A Langevin limit from one explicit deterministic interacting bath

UNSOLVED
Fields
Stochastic processes from deterministic dynamics
Topics
Brownian motionLangevin equationheavy tracerHamiltonian bathGreen–Kubo friction

Problem

Assumptions

One explicit translation-invariant interacting bath and a specified thermodynamic or Rayleigh limit order.

Claim

Construct one explicit translation-invariant deterministic Hamiltonian bath with finite-range nonlinear interactions, an equilibrium state at temperature T, and a smooth finite-range coupling to a tracer of mass M. Exclude harmonic baths, ideal gases, and baths with externally imposed stochastic noise.

For that model, prove that under a stated Rayleigh scaling of time and momentum the tracer velocity converges in law, as M→∞ and the bath volume tends to infinity in a stated order, to

dVτ=−γVτdτ+√(2γT)dBτ,dV_τ=-γV_τ dτ+√(2γT)dB_τ,dVτ​=−γVτ​dτ+√(2γT)dBτ​,

where 0<γ<∞ and γ equals the corresponding Green–Kubo force-autocorrelation integral.
