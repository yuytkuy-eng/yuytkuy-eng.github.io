Wigner crystallization in three-dimensional quantum jellium

UNSOLVED
Fields
Quantum Coulomb matter
Topics
jelliumWigner crystallow densitytranslation symmetry breaking

Problem

Assumptions

Three-dimensional quantum spin-one-half electrons at low density; use a thermodynamic diffraction or crystal-state criterion.

Claim

For three-dimensional quantum jellium at zero temperature, prove that at sufficiently low density there exists an infinite-volume ground state with crystalline order. A concrete target is a nonzero Bragg peak in the static structure factor at some reciprocal vector k≠0. A stronger version identifies the body-centered-cubic lattice.
