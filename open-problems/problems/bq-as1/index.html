Problem

Definitions

A locally conformally symplectic structure has nondegenerate ω\omegaω and a closed Lee form η\etaη with dω=η∧ωd\omega=\eta\wedge\omegadω=η∧ω. Put dη=d−η∧d_\eta=d-\eta\wedgedη​=d−η∧. A nonzero Lee class cannot be removed by a global conformal rescaling.

Assumptions

A finite-dimensional state manifold and physically defined variables; nonzero Lee class.

Claim

Construct a physical model with a derived action or constitutive law and observable consequences realizing this structure.
