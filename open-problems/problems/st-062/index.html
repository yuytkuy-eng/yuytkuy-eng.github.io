Gaplessness of the two-dimensional Gaffnian parent Hamiltonian

UNSOLVED
Fields
Fractional quantum Hall states
Topics

Problem

Assumptions

Even particle number and Gaffnian flux Nϕ=3N/2−3N_\phi=3N/2-3Nϕ​=3N/2−3, with fixed coefficients.

Claim

For the standard bosonic Gaffnian parent Hamiltonian at filling ν=2/3, in a fixed two-dimensional thermodynamic geometry, prove that its smallest positive eigenvalue tends to zero as particle number tends to infinity. Preferably give an explicit normalized variational sequence Ψ_N orthogonal to the zero-mode space with

⟨ΨN,HNΨN⟩→0.⟨Ψ_N,H_NΨ_N⟩ → 0.⟨ΨN​,HN​ΨN​⟩→0.
