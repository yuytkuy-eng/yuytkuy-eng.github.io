Two-way capacities of Gaussian channels

UNSOLVED
Fields
Quantum InformationQuantum Communication
Topics
channels

Problem

Definitions

Attenuator and amplifier Heisenberg relations are aout=ηa+1−ηba_{\rm out}=\sqrt\eta a+\sqrt{1-\eta}baout​=η​a+1−η​b and aout=κa+κ−1b†a_{\rm out}=\sqrt\kappa a+\sqrt{\kappa-1}b^\daggeraout​=κ​a+κ−1​b†, with specified Gaussian environment. Two-way rates are Bell-pair or secret-bit yields per channel use at vanishing error.

Assumptions

Fix all model parameters and an average photon budget before block length tends to infinity; the unconstrained version takes the supremum over budgets.

Claim

Determine exact two-way entanglement-distribution capacity Q2Q_2Q2​ and secret-key capacity KKK for specified noisy thermal-loss, thermal-amplifier and additive-noise channels, distinguishing unrestricted-energy from fixed-energy coding.
