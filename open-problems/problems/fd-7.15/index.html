Finite-dimensional geometric quantization with hyperbolic and focus-focus polarizations

UNSOLVED
Fields
Hamiltonian MechanicsIntegrable SystemsSymplectic Geometry
Topics
geometric quantizationsheaf cohomologysingular polarization

Problem

Definitions

A prequantum line bundle has curvature −iω-i\omega−iω. For a singular real polarization P\mathcal PP, the sheaf J\mathcal JJ of leafwise flat sections defines Q=⨁kHk(M,J)Q=\bigoplus_kH^k(M,\mathcal J)Q=⨁k​Hk(M,J).

Assumptions

Hyperbolic and focus-focus singularities; preserve the stated invariants and the regular and elliptic models.

Claim

Construct a natural finite-dimensional modified quantization, prove its functorial or equivalence properties and relate singular contributions to integrable-system invariants.
