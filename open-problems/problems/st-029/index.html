Ensemble noninteracting v-representability of physical Coulomb densities

CLAIMED
Fields
Density functional theory
Topics
Kohn-Sham DFTv-representabilityground-state densityinverse problem

Problem

Definitions

Let ρ be an ensemble ground-state density of an N-electron Coulomb Hamiltonian with external potential v∈L^{3/2}(R^3)+L^∞(R^3), finite kinetic energy, and ∫ρ=N.

Assumptions

Three-dimensional physical Coulomb ground-state ensembles with finite kinetic energy, including degeneracy.

Claim

Decide whether ρ must be ensemble noninteracting v-representable: must there exist v_s in the same potential class such that ρ is the density of a ground-state ensemble of

∑i=1N(−Δi+vs(xi))?\sum_{i=1}^N (-Δ_i+v_s(x_i))?i=1∑N​(−Δi​+vs​(xi​))?

Prove the assertion for this physical class or give one explicit counterexample.
