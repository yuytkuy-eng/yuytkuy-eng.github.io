Noncryptographic classical verification of BQP

UNSOLVED
Fields
Quantum ComputingComputational Complexity
Topics
complexity

Problem

Definitions

Use uniform polynomial-size circuits and input bit length as the complexity parameter. BQP has no witness; QMA has a polynomial-qubit witness; QCMA has a polynomial-bit witness; QMA(2) has two unentangled quantum witnesses. Standard bounded-error verification has completeness at least 2/32/32/3 and soundness at most 1/31/31/3.

Assumptions

One quantum prover, a classical polynomial-time verifier and polynomially many classical interactions; information-theoretic soundness against an unbounded prover.

Claim

Does every BQP decision problem admit an interactive proof with one polynomial-time honest quantum prover and a probabilistic polynomial-time classical verifier, using polynomially many rounds of classical messages, completeness at least 2/32/32/3 and soundness at most 1/31/31/3 against an unrestricted cheating prover? Require no computational hardness assumption, trusted quantum setup or additional noncommunicating prover.
