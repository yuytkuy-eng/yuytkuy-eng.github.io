Problem

Definitions

For a strictly convex curve γ\gammaγ, the outer billiard map is T(x)=2p−xT(x)=2p-xT(x)=2p−x, where ppp is the consistently oriented tangency point from xxx to γ\gammaγ. An essential invariant curve winds once around γ\gammaγ.

On S2S^2S2 or H2H^2H2, use geodesic tangency and midpoint reflection; algebraicity is defined in the specified standard embedding.

Assumptions

A nontrivial algebraic integral on the domain where the map is defined.

Claim

Must the boundary be the corresponding quadratic curve?
