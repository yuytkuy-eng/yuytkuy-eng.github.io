A nonperturbative construction of quantum gravity

UNSOLVED
Fields
General RelativityMathematical Cosmology
Topics
quantum gravitynonperturbative constructionultraviolet completion

Problem

Definitions

Specify a state space, observable algebra, dynamics, diffeomorphism constraints and an Einstein–matter low-energy effective limit.

Assumptions

Fix Lorentzian or Euclidean formulation, ultraviolet definition and semiclassical regime.

Claim

Construct a mathematically consistent ultraviolet-complete theory, prove constraint and anomaly control and unitarity, and recover the correct low-energy limit.
