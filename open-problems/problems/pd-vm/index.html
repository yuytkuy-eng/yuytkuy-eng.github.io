Take x,p∈R3x,p\in\mathbb R^3x,p∈R3, smooth compactly supported f0≥0f_0\ge0f0​≥0, and smooth electromagnetic fields satisfying the Gauss constraints and finite total energy. The particle distribution may be arbitrarily large; nonzero total charge permits a noncompact electric field.

Claim

Does every admissible initial datum generate a global classical solution? Equivalently, can the momentum support remain bounded on every finite time interval, or can admissible data develop a singularity?

Progress

  • Bigorgne's 2025 theorem establishes global existence and modified scattering for a small particle distribution while relaxing smallness assumptions on the electromagnetic field. The unrestricted large-distribution problem remains open.
