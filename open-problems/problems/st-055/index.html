Derivation of the phonon Boltzmann equation from a three-dimensional anharmonic crystal

UNSOLVED
Fields
Kinetic theory and transport
Topics
phononsBoltzmann equationanharmonic crystalkinetic limitWigner function

Problem

Assumptions

Regular initial Wigner profile, explicit mixing and cumulant bounds, stable potential and nondegenerate collision resonances; kinetic scaling t=λ−2τt=\lambda^{-2}\taut=λ−2τ, x=λ−2rx=\lambda^{-2}rx=λ−2r.

Claim

Fix a three-dimensional Bravais lattice with a stable harmonic acoustic dispersion and a generic weak cubic or quartic anharmonicity λV. For slowly varying centered random initial data with prescribed Wigner function W_0(r,k), prove that on kinetic scales t=λ^{-2}τ and x=λ^{-2}r the microscopic Wigner distribution converges, for τ in a nonzero interval, to the solution of the spatially inhomogeneous phonon Boltzmann equation with the corresponding three- or four-phonon collision operator.
