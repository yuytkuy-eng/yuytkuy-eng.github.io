Existence, uniqueness, and complete-basis convergence of exact-exchange OEP

UNSOLVED
Fields
Density functional theory and electronic structure
Topics
optimized effective potentialexact exchangeexistenceuniquenessbasis convergenceKohn-Sham

Problem

Definitions

Fix a finite Coulomb molecule and electron number for which the occupied Kohn–Sham spectrum is nondegenerate and gapped. Minimize the exchange-only Kohn–Sham energy over multiplicative potentials v_s in a stated continuum space, modulo constants.

Assumptions

Finite Coulomb molecule with gapped orbitals, an admissible potential class and stable nested orbital and potential bases.

Claim

Prove that a minimizer exists and is unique modulo a constant. Then prove that Galerkin OEP solutions obtained from nested orbital and potential bases converge to it in a specified norm, with no spurious null-space oscillations.
