Problem

Definitions

Use a time-oriented four-dimensional Lorentzian spacetime of signature (−,+,+,+)(-,+,+,+)(−,+,+,+) satisfying G(g)+Λg=8πTG(g)+\Lambda g=8\pi TG(g)+Λg=8πT. Initial data (Σ,h,K)(\Sigma,h,K)(Σ,h,K) obey R(h)+(tr⁡K)2−∣K∣2=16πμ+2ΛR(h)+(\operatorname{tr}K)^2-|K|^2=16\pi\mu+2\LambdaR(h)+(trK)2−∣K∣2=16πμ+2Λ and div⁡(K−(tr⁡K)h)=8πJ\operatorname{div}(K-(\operatorname{tr}K)h)=8\pi Jdiv(K−(trK)h)=8πJ. The MGHD is the maximal globally hyperbolic development.

A spike is a narrowing concentration of normalized spatial derivatives where neighboring timelines undergo different Kasner-transition sequences.

Assumptions

Fix matter, symmetry, genericity and definitions of width, recurrence and support dimension.

Claim

Construct generic spike evolutions and their statistical laws, determine support measure and their effect on almost-everywhere BKL asymptotics.
