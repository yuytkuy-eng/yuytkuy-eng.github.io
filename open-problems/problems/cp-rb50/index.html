Problem

Definitions

mB(γ,H)m_B(\gamma,H)mB​(γ,H) is the infimum of ADM masses of admissible asymptotically flat extensions with R≥0R\ge0R≥0, matched boundary data and no additional enclosing minimal horizon. Static vacuum obeys NRic⁡(h)=Hess⁡NN\operatorname{Ric}(h)=\operatorname{Hess}NNRic(h)=HessN, ΔN=0\Delta N=0ΔN=0.

Assumptions

Fix the admissibility version, positive boundary mean curvature, regularity and nonempty extension class.

Claim

Prove that the infimum is attained by a unique static vacuum extension, or determine the precise replacement in nonattainment or nonuniqueness cases.
