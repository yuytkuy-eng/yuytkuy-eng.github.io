A non-Gaussian subsequential scaling limit of the three-dimensional Ising magnetization field

UNSOLVED
Fields
Critical phenomena and constructive field theory
Topics
three-dimensional Ising modelscaling limittightnessnon-Gaussian fieldfourth cumulant

Problem

Definitions

At the critical inverse temperature of the nearest-neighbour Ising model on Z^3, form the lattice magnetization distribution Φ_a by lattice spacing a and choose its normalization so that Var[Φ_a(f_0)]=1 for one fixed nonzero test function f_0.

Assumptions

Critical nearest-neighbor zero-field model on Z3\mathbb Z^3Z3 with an explicit local distribution topology.

Claim

Prove tightness of {Φ_a}_{a↓0} in H^{-s}_loc for some explicit s, extract a nonzero subsequential limit Φ, and prove that Φ is not Gaussian—for example by showing that one connected four-point functional of Φ is nonzero.
