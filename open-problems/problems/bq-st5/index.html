Definitions

For a smooth hypersurface germ in (R2n,ω0)(\mathbb R^{2n},\omega_0)(R2n,ω0​), the characteristic line is ker⁡(ω0∣TxM)\ker(\omega_0|_{T_xM})ker(ω0​∣Tx​M​).

Assumptions

Every maximal local characteristic curve lies in an affine two-plane.

Claim

Classify the local geometric or symplectic possibilities, including nonconvex germs.
