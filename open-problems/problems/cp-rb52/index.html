Positivity of Bartnik and Penrose quasilocal masses

UNSOLVED
Fields
General RelativityMathematical Cosmology
Topics
quasi-local mass positivityBartnikPenrose mass

Problem

Definitions

Use the chosen nonnegative-scalar-curvature Bartnik extension definition and a separately specified two-surface twistor definition of Penrose mass.

Assumptions

The enclosed region is not locally Euclidean; specify boundary, energy and twistor hypotheses.

Claim

Prove strict Bartnik positivity and zero-mass rigidity, and nonnegativity or counterexamples for the specified Penrose mass.
