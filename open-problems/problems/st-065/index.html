Pure-state noninteracting v-representability of physical Coulomb densities

UNSOLVED
Fields
Density functional theory
Topics
Kohn-Sham DFTpure-state v-representabilityinteger occupationsinverse Kohn-Sham problem

Problem

Definitions

Let ρ be the density of a nondegenerate, spin-unpolarized ground state of an N-electron Coulomb Hamiltonian with an admissible molecular external potential.

Assumptions

Compatible particle number, real spin-independent potentials and nondegenerate physical ground states.

Claim

Decide whether there always exists a local scalar potential v_s such that ρ is produced by a single noninteracting ground-state Slater determinant with integer occupations. Require v_s∈L^{3/2}+L^∞ and specify treatment of the additive constant. Prove the assertion or give one physical Coulomb counterexample.
