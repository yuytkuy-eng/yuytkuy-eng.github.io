Approximate average-case hardness for BosonSampling

UNSOLVED
Fields
Quantum ComputingComputational Complexity
Topics
complexity

Problem

Definitions

Use uniform polynomial-size circuits and input bit length as the complexity parameter. BQP has no witness; QMA has a polynomial-qubit witness; QCMA has a polynomial-bit witness; QMA(2) has two unentangled quantum witnesses. Standard bounded-error verification has completeness at least 2/32/32/3 and soundness at most 1/31/31/3.

Standard boson-sampling probabilities involve squared permanents of Gaussian submatrices.

Assumptions

Quantified average-case error and anticoncentration, with inverse-polynomial total-variation sampling accuracy.

Claim

Prove average-case approximate hardness for complex-Gaussian permanents, together with a robust reduction strong enough to rule out polynomial-time classical sampling within inverse-polynomial total-variation error for standard BosonSampling under standard worst-case complexity assumptions.
