Problem

Definitions

A symmetric covariant tensor KKK of rank ddd is Killing if ∇(i0Ki1⋯id)=0\nabla_{(i_0}K_{i_1\cdots i_d)}=0∇(i0​​Ki1​⋯id​)​=0. It gives the geodesic integral K(γ˙,…,γ˙)K(\dot\gamma,\ldots,\dot\gamma)K(γ˙​,…,γ˙​). Reducible tensors lie in the constant-coefficient algebra generated by the metric and lower-rank Killing tensors under symmetric tensor products.

A differential quantization F^\widehat FF has principal symbol FFF and may include lower-order corrections; exact quantization requires [Δg,F^]=0[\Delta_g,\widehat F]=0[Δg​,F]=0.

Assumptions

A Riemannian metric, all global polynomial geodesic integrals and a fixed function or density convention.

Claim

Classify metrics for which every such integral admits an exact commuting quantization, using curvature or cohomological criteria or explicit higher-order anomalies.
