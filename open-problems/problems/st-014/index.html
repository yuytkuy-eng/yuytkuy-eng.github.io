The Nelson–Kosterlitz universal jump in the two-dimensional XY model

UNSOLVED
Fields
Classical statistical mechanics
Topics
XY modelBKT transitionhelicity modulusuniversal jump

Problem

Definitions

For the nearest-neighbor classical XY model on Z², define the infinite-volume helicity modulus Υ(T) by the second derivative of the free energy with respect to a uniform boundary twist.

Assumptions

Nearest-neighbor square-lattice cosine coupling one; the thermodynamic limit precedes approach to critical temperature.

Claim

Prove that there is a BKT transition temperature T_c and that

Υ(Tc−)=2Tc/π,Υ(T_c−) = 2T_c/π,Υ(Tc​−)=2Tc​/π,

while Υ(T)=0 for T>T_c.
