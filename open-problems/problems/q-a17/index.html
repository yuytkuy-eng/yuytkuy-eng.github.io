Problem

Definitions

Use uniform polynomial-size circuits and input bit length as the complexity parameter. BQP has no witness; QMA has a polynomial-qubit witness; QCMA has a polynomial-bit witness; QMA(2) has two unentangled quantum witnesses. Standard bounded-error verification has completeness at least 2/32/32/3 and soundness at most 1/31/31/3.

Identity testing distinguishes a known target σ\sigmaσ from an unknown copied state ρ\rhoρ at a prescribed trace-distance gap.

Assumptions

Adaptive single-qubit Pauli measurements, no entangling measurements within or across copies and success probability at least 2/32/32/3.

Claim

Given a full classical description of an arbitrary reference state σ\sigmaσ and copies of an unknown NNN-qubit state ρ\rhoρ, determine the optimal copy complexity of testing ρ=σ\rho=\sigmaρ=σ versus ∥ρ−σ∥1≥ε\|\rho-\sigma\|_1\ge\varepsilon∥ρ−σ∥1​≥ε, with success at least 2/32/32/3, using adaptive single-qubit Pauli measurements and no entangling measurements within or across copies. Seek matching instance-dependent bounds and the accompanying classical running time.
