In a planar billiard table Ω⊂R2\Omega\subset\mathbb R^2Ω⊂R2, a unit-speed particle moves along straight lines and reflects by v↦v−2⟨v,n⟩nv\mapsto v-2\langle v,n\rangle nv↦v−2⟨v,n⟩n.
