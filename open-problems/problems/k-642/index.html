Definitions

The Kerr interior is r−<r<r+r_-<r<r_+r−​<r<r+​. A CrC^rCr extension through the Cauchy horizon requires a nondegenerate Lorentzian metric in that class on a larger manifold. Continuous, Lipschitz, twice differentiable and locally square-integrable-connection extension criteria are distinct.

The coupled model is linearized Einstein–Maxwell around Kerr–Newman.

Assumptions

A fixed subextremal or explicitly restricted charge–rotation regime and generic horizon lower bounds.

Claim
