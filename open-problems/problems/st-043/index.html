Saturated ferromagnetism at finite hole density in the U=∞ Hubbard model

UNSOLVED
Fields
Strongly correlated electrons
Topics
Hubbard modelNagaoka ferromagnetisminfinite Ukinetic magnetism

Problem

Definitions

Consider the U=∞ Hubbard model on the square lattice: nearest-neighbour hopping of electrons with double occupancy projected out, at electron density 1−δ.

Assumptions

Two-dimensional infinite-UUU Hubbard model at fixed positive hole density in the thermodynamic limit.

Claim

Determine whether Nagaoka ferromagnetism survives finite doping: prove that there exists δ_c>0 such that for all 0<δ<δ_c the thermodynamic ground state is fully spin polarized, with total spin density (1−δ)/2; or prove that for every δ>0 the fully polarized state fails to be a ground state in the thermodynamic limit.
