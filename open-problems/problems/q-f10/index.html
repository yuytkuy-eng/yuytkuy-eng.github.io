Quantum causal models assign CP maps and latent states to a specified graph; interventions replace channels. An effect is identifiable if observationally equivalent models agree on it.

Assumptions

Fixed graph, latent variables, accessible interventions and sample or noise norm.

Claim

For a specified quantum causal graph with hidden systems and restricted interventions, characterize which causal effects are identifiable and derive complete inference procedures and sample-complexity bounds, or prove observational indistinguishability.
