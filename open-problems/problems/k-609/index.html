Problem

Definitions

In fixed data and solution spaces X,YX,YX,Y, nonlinear asymptotic stability means that every sufficiently small constraint-compatible perturbation has a complete future exterior and converges, modulo gauge, to a nearby stationary endstate. Unless uniformity is explicitly required, the smallness threshold and estimates may depend on the fixed background.

The Teukolsky equations Tsψs=0T_s\psi_s=0Ts​ψs​=0 govern separated spin fields; s=±2s=\pm2s=±2 describes extreme Weyl components of linearized gravity. Metric stability also requires reconstruction and control of parameter and gauge modes.

Assumptions

Every fixed subextremal Kerr–de Sitter background with explicit mode-stability, constraint, gauge and spectral-gap hypotheses.

Claim

Prove conditional nonlinear stability from those linear hypotheses or identify iteration and gauge obstructions.
