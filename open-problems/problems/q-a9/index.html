StoqMA versus MA and error amplification

UNSOLVED
Fields
Quantum ComputingComputational Complexity
Topics
complexity

Problem

Definitions

Use uniform polynomial-size circuits and input bit length as the complexity parameter. BQP has no witness; QMA has a polynomial-qubit witness; QCMA has a polynomial-bit witness; QMA(2) has two unentangled quantum witnesses. Standard bounded-error verification has completeness at least 2/32/32/3 and soundness at most 1/31/31/3.

A StoqMA verifier uses reversible classical gates, zero and plus ancillas and a final XXX measurement, with acceptance thresholds c,sc,sc,s.

Assumptions

1/2≤s<c≤11/2\le s<c\le11/2≤s<c≤1, inverse-polynomial gap and efficiently specified thresholds; amplification retains legal StoqMA operations.

Claim

Is StoqMA(c,s)=MA\mathsf{StoqMA}(c,s)=\mathsf{MA}StoqMA(c,s)=MA? Equivalently, can every polynomial ℓ\ellℓ amplify to completeness 1−2−ℓ1-2^{-\ell}1−2−ℓ and soundness 1/2+2−ℓ1/2+2^{-\ell}1/2+2−ℓ?
