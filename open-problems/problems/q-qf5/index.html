Problem

Definitions

Fix Wightman fields, a Haag–Kastler local net or Osterwalder–Schrader Schwinger functions, including covariance, locality, vacuum and positive-energy axioms. A Hamiltonian mass gap means spec⁡H∩(0,Δ)=∅\operatorname{spec}H\cap(0,\Delta)=\varnothingspecH∩(0,Δ)=∅ for some Δ>0\Delta>0Δ>0.

An anomaly-free chiral representation cancels local triangle and global anomalies; a chosen lattice Dirac regulator may satisfy Ginsparg–Wilson conditions.

Assumptions

Non-Abelian theory, cutoff-uniform locality, mirror decoupling and prescribed continuum fermions.

Claim

Construct nonperturbative regulators for non-Abelian chiral gauge theories with all local and global anomalies cancelled, proving locality, global gauge consistency, removal of unwanted mirror modes and the required continuum fermion content.
