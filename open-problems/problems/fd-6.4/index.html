Action-angle coordinates for splittable singular Poisson systems

UNSOLVED
Fields
Hamiltonian MechanicsIntegrable SystemsSymplectic Geometry
Topics
splittable integrable systemsingular action-angle

Problem

Definitions

On an mmm-manifold with a Poisson tensor of generic rank 2r2r2r, an integrable map F=(f1,…,fm−r)F=(f_1,\ldots,f_{m-r})F=(f1​,…,fm−r​) has independent differentials and pairwise Poisson-commuting components. Its compact joint Hamiltonian leaves are rrr-tori. At regular points, action-angle coordinates give Π=∑i=1r∂θi∧∂Ii\Pi=\sum_{i=1}^r\partial_{\theta_i}\wedge\partial_{I_i}Π=∑i=1r​∂θi​​∧∂Ii​​, with transverse Casimir coordinates.

Splittability means simultaneous decomposition of the Poisson structure and integral map into symplectic and transverse parts.

Assumptions

A compact invariant torus in the splittable class, with specified transverse stability or compact-group hypotheses.

Claim

Establish action-angle forms with a transverse Poisson component and determine necessary and sufficient conditions.
