Problem

Definitions

Use a time-oriented four-dimensional Lorentzian spacetime of signature (−,+,+,+)(-,+,+,+)(−,+,+,+) satisfying G(g)+Λg=8πTG(g)+\Lambda g=8\pi TG(g)+Λg=8πT. Initial data (Σ,h,K)(\Sigma,h,K)(Σ,h,K) obey R(h)+(tr⁡K)2−∣K∣2=16πμ+2ΛR(h)+(\operatorname{tr}K)^2-|K|^2=16\pi\mu+2\LambdaR(h)+(trK)2−∣K∣2=16πμ+2Λ and div⁡(K−(tr⁡K)h)=8πJ\operatorname{div}(K-(\operatorname{tr}K)h)=8\pi Jdiv(K−(trK)h)=8πJ. The MGHD is the maximal globally hyperbolic development.

The local BKL ODE is obtained by deleting Hubble-normalized spatial-gradient terms from the evolution equations.

Assumptions

Four-dimensional vacuum spacelike singularities; fixed foliation, data topology and spatial measure, allowing spike exceptions.

Claim

Prove decay of normalized spatial gradients along almost every timeline and control the asymptotic PDE–ODE difference and Kasner transitions.
