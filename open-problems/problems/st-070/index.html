The leading correction to local-density exchange in large-Z neutral atoms

UNSOLVED
Fields
Density functional theory and atomic asymptotics
Topics
large-Z atomsexchange energylocal density approximationZ log Zshell effects

Problem

Definitions

Let ρ_Z be the exact ground-state density of the neutral atom with N=Z and let E_x[ρ_Z] be exchange defined by the right derivative at zero coupling in the adiabatic-connection constrained search. It is expected that

Ex[ρZ]=−AxZ5/3+Rx(Z),E_x[ρ_Z]=-A_x Z^{5/3}+R_x(Z),Ex​[ρZ​]=−Ax​Z5/3+Rx​(Z),

with A_x given by Dirac exchange.

Assumptions

Large nuclear charge along a specified closed-shell or averaged sequence, retaining Kohn–Sham representability requirements.

Claim

Determine the correct leading asymptotic of R_x(Z), including its coefficient. To suppress shell oscillations, use either a specified Cesàro average in Z or an explicitly defined closed-shell subsequence; decide in that convention whether a Z log Z term is present.
