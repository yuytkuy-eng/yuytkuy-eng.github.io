Quantum search-to-decision reductions

UNSOLVED
Fields
Quantum ComputingComputational Complexity
Topics
complexity

Problem

Definitions

Use uniform polynomial-size circuits and input bit length as the complexity parameter. BQP has no witness; QMA has a polynomial-qubit witness; QCMA has a polynomial-bit witness; QMA(2) has two unentangled quantum witnesses. Standard bounded-error verification has completeness at least 2/32/32/3 and soundness at most 1/31/31/3.

Witness search asks for a state accepted with probability at least c−1/poly⁡(n)c-1/\operatorname{poly}(n)c−1/poly(n).

Assumptions

Fix a coherent QMA decision oracle and its behavior on inputs outside the promise.

Claim

Can a polynomial-time quantum algorithm, with polynomially many coherent queries to a QMA-complete decision oracle, prepare an accepting witness for every yes-instance of a given QMA verifier? Require bounded overall failure probability and inverse-polynomial accuracy, under the standard promise-oracle convention.
