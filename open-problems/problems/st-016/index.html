Equilibrium crystallization of three-dimensional hard spheres

UNSOLVED
Fields
Classical statistical mechanics
Topics
crystallizationhard spheresGibbs measuressymmetry breaking

Problem

Assumptions

Finite inverse temperature and finite positive activity; specify translation-breaking states or a nonzero Bragg criterion.

Claim

Prove that the three-dimensional hard-sphere model has a finite-density Gibbs state that breaks continuous translation symmetry. A sufficient formulation is: show that for some activity z<∞ there exists an infinite-volume Gibbs measure whose pair correlation function has a nonzero Bragg component at some wavevector k≠0.
