Classify complex Hadamard matrices of order six

CLAIMED
Fields
Finite-dimensional harmonic analysis
Topics
complex Hadamard matricesclassificationMUBalgebraic geometry

Problem

Assumptions

All complex Hadamard matrices of order six.

Claim

Let H∈C^{6×6} satisfy |H_{jk}|=1 and HH*=6I. Classify all such H up to left and right multiplication by diagonal unitary and permutation matrices.

A concrete form is to prove or disprove the proposed exhaustive description by Szöllősi's four-parameter family, its degenerate/Karlsson strata, and Tao's isolated spectral matrix.
