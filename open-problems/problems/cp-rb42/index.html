Nongenericity of extensions through compact Cauchy horizons

UNSOLVED

Problem

Definitions

Use a time-oriented four-dimensional Lorentzian spacetime of signature (−,+,+,+)(-,+,+,+)(−,+,+,+) satisfying G(g)+Λg=8πTG(g)+\Lambda g=8\pi TG(g)+Λg=8πT. Initial data (Σ,h,K)(\Sigma,h,K)(Σ,h,K) obey R(h)+(tr⁡K)2−∣K∣2=16πμ+2ΛR(h)+(\operatorname{tr}K)^2-|K|^2=16\pi\mu+2\LambdaR(h)+(trK)2−∣K∣2=16πμ+2Λ and div⁡(K−(tr⁡K)h)=8πJ\operatorname{div}(K-(\operatorname{tr}K)h)=8\pi Jdiv(K−(trK)h)=8πJ. The MGHD is the maximal globally hyperbolic development.

Genericity is taken in a specified constrained CkC^kCk data space; a compact Cauchy horizon is nonempty.

Assumptions

Fixed compact Σ\SigmaΣ; separate smooth and distributional extensions and degenerate horizons.

Claim

Prove that data admitting extension through a compact Cauchy horizon are nongeneric and determine low-regularity and zero-surface-gravity exceptions.
