A gauge-invariant Hohenberg–Kohn theorem for physical current density

UNSOLVED
Fields
Current-density functional theory
Topics
CDFTmagnetic fieldsphysical currentgauge invarianceHohenberg-Kohn theorem

Problem

Definitions

Let H(v,A) be the N-electron Coulomb Hamiltonian with smooth decaying scalar and vector potentials on R^3, and assume a nondegenerate ground state.

Assumptions

Three-dimensional nondegenerate fixed-particle ground states, regular potentials and magnetic gauge quotient; use physical current.

Claim

Decide whether the pair consisting of particle density ρ and physical current

j=jp+ρAj=j_p+ρAj=jp​+ρA

determines (v,A) up to the usual static gauge transformation and an additive scalar constant. Prove injectivity for one explicit open class of fields, or give two gauge-inequivalent field pairs in that class with the same (ρ,j).
