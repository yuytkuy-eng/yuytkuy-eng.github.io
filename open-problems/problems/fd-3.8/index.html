Does first-order curvature homogeneity imply local homogeneity?

Problem

Definitions

A metric is kkk-curvature homogeneous if for every x,yx,yx,y a linear isometry matches (∇jR)x(\nabla^jR)_x(∇jR)x​ and (∇jR)y(\nabla^jR)_y(∇jR)y​ for 0≤j≤k0\le j\le k0≤j≤k. Local homogeneity means that local isometry orbits are open.

Assumptions

Positive-definite Riemannian metrics in arbitrary dimension.
