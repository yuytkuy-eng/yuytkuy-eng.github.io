Problem

Definitions

In effective field theory, Λeff=Λbare+8πGρvac\Lambda_{\rm eff}=\Lambda_{\rm bare}+8\pi G\rho_{\rm vac}Λeff​=Λbare​+8πGρvac​, with threshold corrections of order m4m^4m4. Radiative stability means no repeated fine adjustment is required to retain small Λ\LambdaΛ.

Assumptions

Fix the ultraviolet theory, couplings, matter spectrum and observable definition.

Claim

Derive a small positive cosmological constant through a radiatively stable mechanism and establish its quantum corrections and testable consequences.
