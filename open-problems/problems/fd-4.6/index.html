Problem

Definitions

Let Ω⊂R2\Omega\subset\mathbb R^2Ω⊂R2 have smooth strictly convex boundary with positive curvature. The billiard map TTT acts on S1×(0,π)S^1\times(0,\pi)S1×(0,π) by specular reflection. A convex caustic is a closed convex curve whose tangent trajectories remain tangent after reflection. A rational caustic of rotation number p/qp/qp/q corresponds to an essential invariant circle Γ\GammaΓ with Tq∣Γ=IdT^q|_\Gamma=\mathrm{Id}Tq∣Γ​=Id and winding number ppp.

Assumptions

The table has reflection symmetry in two perpendicular axes and a two-periodic orbit along one axis.
