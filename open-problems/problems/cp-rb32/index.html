Problem

Definitions

Use a time-oriented four-dimensional Lorentzian spacetime of signature (−,+,+,+)(-,+,+,+)(−,+,+,+) satisfying G(g)+Λg=8πTG(g)+\Lambda g=8\pi TG(g)+Λg=8πT. Initial data (Σ,h,K)(\Sigma,h,K)(Σ,h,K) obey R(h)+(tr⁡K)2−∣K∣2=16πμ+2ΛR(h)+(\operatorname{tr}K)^2-|K|^2=16\pi\mu+2\LambdaR(h)+(trK)2−∣K∣2=16πμ+2Λ and div⁡(K−(tr⁡K)h)=8πJ\operatorname{div}(K-(\operatorname{tr}K)h)=8\pi Jdiv(K−(trK)h)=8πJ. The MGHD is the maximal globally hyperbolic development.

The dominant energy condition requires −Tμνvν-T^\mu{}_\nu v^\nu−Tμν​vν to be future causal for every future timelike vvv. Timelike convergence means Ric⁡(v,v)≥0\operatorname{Ric}(v,v)\ge0Ric(v,v)≥0 for timelike vvv; null convergence means Ric⁡(k,k)≥0\operatorname{Ric}(k,k)\ge0Ric(k,k)≥0 for null kkk. These are distinct hypotheses.

Assumptions

Global hyperbolicity, compact Cauchy surfaces, timelike convergence and two-sided timelike geodesic completeness.

Claim

Prove (M,g)≅(R×Σ,−dt2+h)(M,g)\cong(\mathbb R\times\Sigma,-dt^2+h)(M,g)≅(R×Σ,−dt2+h) for some compact Riemannian (Σ,h)(\Sigma,h)(Σ,h).
