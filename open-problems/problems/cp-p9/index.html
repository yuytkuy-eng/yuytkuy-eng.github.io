Complete classification of higher-dimensional black holes

UNSOLVED
Fields

Topics

Problem

Definitions

Consider stationary asymptotically flat vacuum black holes in dimension D≥5D\ge5D≥5 with compact horizon sections; the DOC is the domain of outer communications.

Assumptions

Fix horizon nondegeneracy, regularity, axial symmetries, DOC topology and candidate mass, angular-momentum and rod invariants.

Claim

Give a complete classification and necessary and sufficient uniqueness data.
