Exponential clustering at all temperatures in the two-dimensional classical Heisenberg model

UNSOLVED
Fields
Classical statistical mechanics
Topics
O(3) modelasymptotic freedommass gapcorrelation decaytwo dimensions

Problem

Assumptions

Two dimensions, every finite inverse temperature, zero field and unit coupling.

Claim

For the nearest-neighbour classical Heisenberg (O(3)) model on Z² with Gibbs weight exp(β Σ_{⟨x,y⟩} σ_x·σ_y), σ_x∈S², prove that for every β<∞ the two-point function decays exponentially,

⟨σ0⋅σx⟩β≤C(β)e−∣x∣/ξ(β),ξ(β)<∞;⟨σ_0·σ_x⟩_β ≤ C(β) e^{−|x|/ξ(β)}, ξ(β)<∞;⟨σ0​⋅σx​⟩β​≤C(β)e−∣x∣/ξ(β),ξ(β)<∞;

or disprove this by establishing a phase with slower-than-exponential decay at large β.
