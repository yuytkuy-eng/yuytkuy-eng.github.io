NP versus BQP

UNSOLVED
Fields
Quantum ComputingComputational Complexity
Topics
complexity

Problem

Definitions

Use uniform polynomial-size circuits and input bit length as the complexity parameter. BQP has no witness; QMA has a polynomial-qubit witness; QCMA has a polynomial-bit witness; QMA(2) has two unentangled quantum witnesses. Standard bounded-error verification has completeness at least 2/32/32/3 and soundness at most 1/31/31/3.

NP has polynomial-length classical certificates verified in deterministic polynomial time; SAT asks whether a finite Boolean formula has a satisfying assignment.

Assumptions

No oracle or postselection; a fixed efficiently implementable universal gate set.

Claim

Is NP⊆BQP\mathsf{NP}\subseteq\mathsf{BQP}NP⊆BQP? Equivalently, is SAT solvable by a uniform polynomial-time quantum algorithm with error at most 1/31/31/3 on every input?
