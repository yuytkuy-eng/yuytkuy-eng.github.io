Efficient uniform sampling of lattice trees

UNSOLVED

Problem

Definitions

Let Ln\mathcal L_nLn​ be the embedded nearest-neighbor trees in Z2\mathbb Z^2Z2 containing the origin and having nnn vertices. Sample TnT_nTn​ uniformly; diameter is Euclidean.

Assumptions

Rooted square-lattice trees.

Claim

Construct an algorithm whose output is within total variation ε\varepsilonε of uniform on Ln\mathcal L_nLn​ in time polynomial in n,log⁡(1/ε)n,\log(1/\varepsilon)n,log(1/ε), or prove a complexity obstruction.
