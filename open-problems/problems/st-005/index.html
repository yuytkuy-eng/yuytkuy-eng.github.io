A finite-bond-dimension PEPS with a gapped chiral parent Hamiltonian

UNSOLVED
Fields
Tensor networks and topological matter
Topics
PEPSchiral topological orderparent Hamiltonianspectral gap

Problem

Assumptions

Translation-invariant two-dimensional PEPS with finite bond dimension, nonzero chiral central charge and a uniform bulk gap; no opposite-chirality auxiliary layer.

Claim

Decide whether there exists a translation-invariant PEPS of finite bond dimension on a two-dimensional lattice which has nonzero chiral central charge and whose finite-range frustration-free parent Hamiltonian has a uniform bulk spectral gap. Give either an explicit tensor and a certified gap, or a no-go theorem under these assumptions.
