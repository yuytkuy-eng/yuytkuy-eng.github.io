Smooth black-hole uniqueness near Kerr–Newman–de Sitter

UNSOLVED

Problem

Definitions

In fixed data and solution spaces X,YX,YX,Y, nonlinear asymptotic stability means that every sufficiently small constraint-compatible perturbation has a complete future exterior and converges, modulo gauge, to a nearby stationary endstate. Unless uniformity is explicitly required, the smallness threshold and estimates may depend on the fixed background.

The model is stationary Einstein–Maxwell–de Sitter with nonzero charge.

Assumptions

Smooth nonanalytic data near a fixed subextremal Kerr–Newman–de Sitter background and specified event and cosmological horizon hypotheses.

Claim

Prove local rigidity and uniqueness in the stated neighborhood.
