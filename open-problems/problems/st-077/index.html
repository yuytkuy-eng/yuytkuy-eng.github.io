A liquid–gas transition for the hard-core square-well fluid

UNSOLVED

Problem

Definitions

For particles in R³, fix the radial pair potential

φ(r)=+∞for0≤r<1,=−1for1≤r≤3/2,=0forr>3/2.φ(r)=+∞ for 0≤r<1, =-1 for 1≤r≤3/2, = 0 for r>3/2.φ(r)=+∞for0≤r<1,=−1for1≤r≤3/2,=0forr>3/2.

Assumptions

The fixed square-well potential at finite positive temperature and activity; two translation-invariant extremal Gibbs states of different density, both without Bragg order.

Claim

Prove that there exist an inverse temperature β and activity z for which the DLR specification has two distinct extremal translation-invariant Gibbs states μ_g and μ_l satisfying

ρ(μg)<ρ(μl),ρ(μ_g) < ρ(μ_l),ρ(μg​)<ρ(μl​),

and prove that neither state has nonzero Bragg peaks away from wave number zero. Equivalently, establish genuine gas–liquid coexistence rather than merely a gas–crystal transition for this explicit finite-range radial pair potential.
