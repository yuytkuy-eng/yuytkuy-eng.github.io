Compactly supported Nijenhuis operators of prescribed Segre type

Problem

Definitions

For L∈Γ(End⁡TM)L\in\Gamma(\operatorname{End}TM)L∈Γ(EndTM), its Nijenhuis torsion is NL(X,Y)=[LX,LY]−L[LX,Y]−L[X,LY]+L2[X,Y]N_L(X,Y)=[LX,LY]-L[LX,Y]-L[X,LY]+L^2[X,Y]NL​(X,Y)=[LX,LY]−L[LX,Y]−L[X,LY]+L2[X,Y]. A Nijenhuis operator has NL=0N_L=0NL​=0. It is gl-regular when its minimal polynomial has degree dim⁡M\dim MdimM, equivalently each complex eigenvalue has exactly one Jordan block.

Assumptions

LLL is smooth and compactly supported on Rn\mathbb R^nRn, with a fixed Segre type on a nonempty open ball; degeneration to zero is allowed outside.

Claim

Determine which types are realizable, especially a single Jordan block of size at least three.
