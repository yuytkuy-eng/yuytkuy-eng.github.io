Motility-induced phase separation in a hard-core active lattice gas

UNSOLVED

Problem

Definitions

On the torus (Z/LZ)², let each site be empty or contain one particle carrying an orientation u∈{±e_1,±e_2}. Independently, each particle

(i) attempts a symmetric nearest-neighbour exclusion jump at rate 1; (ii) attempts a jump by u at rate v, suppressed if the target is occupied; and (iii) changes to each of the other three orientations at rate D_r/3.

Work in the sector with floor(ρL²) particles and let μ_L be the unique stationary measure. Let K_L={(±2π/L,0),(0,±2π/L)} and define

ML=maxk∈KL∣L−2∑xexp⁡(ik⋅x)(nx−ρ)∣.M_L=max_{k∈K_L} |L^{-2} \sum_x \exp(ik·x)(n_x-ρ)|.ML​=maxk∈KL​​∣L−2x∑​exp(ik⋅x)(nx​−ρ)∣.

Assumptions

Fixed microscopic propulsion and positive rotational diffusion, density strictly between zero and one and thermodynamic limit.

Claim

Prove that there exist explicit v>0, D_r>0, and 0<ρ<1 such that

liminfL→∞EμL[ML2]>0.liminf_{L→∞} E_{μ_L}[M_L²] > 0.liminfL→∞​EμL​​[ML2​]>0.

This is a precise stationary-state signature of motility-induced macroscopic phase separation.
