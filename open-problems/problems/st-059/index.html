Problem

Definitions

In the thermodynamic ground state of spin-unpolarized three-dimensional jellium, let n_{r_s}(k) be the momentum distribution.

Assumptions

Full quantum Coulomb model at every sufficiently small positive rsr_srs​, with thermodynamic limit first.

Claim

Prove that for all sufficiently small density parameter r_s the radial one-sided limits at the interacting Fermi momentum k_F exist and

Z(rs)=limk↑kFnrs(k)−limk↓kFnrs(k)>0.Z(r_s)=lim_{k↑k_F} n_{r_s}(k)-lim_{k↓k_F} n_{r_s}(k) > 0.Z(rs​)=limk↑kF​​nrs​​(k)−limk↓kF​​nrs​​(k)>0.

A theorem may first restrict to a subsequence of cubic volumes with closed noninteracting shells.
