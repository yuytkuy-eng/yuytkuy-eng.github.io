Progress

  • Cylinder DMRG reports a spin liquid with a finite spin gap, consistent with a gapped Z2 phase; finite-circumference extrapolation, not a proof.[1]
  • DMRG flux insertion reports a much smaller spin gap and Dirac-cone signatures, favouring a gapless Dirac spin liquid; the thermodynamic phase remains unresolved.[2]
