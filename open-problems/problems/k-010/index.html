Problem

Definitions

In fixed data and solution spaces X,YX,YX,Y, nonlinear asymptotic stability means that every sufficiently small constraint-compatible perturbation has a complete future exterior and converges, modulo gauge, to a nearby stationary endstate. Unless uniformity is explicitly required, the smallness threshold and estimates may depend on the fixed background.

A geon is horizonless and time-periodic; a black resonator has a positive-area horizon and a helical Killing field.

Assumptions

Four-dimensional vacuum Kerr–AdS with reflecting infinity, specified perturbation norm and unstable modes.

Claim

Classify the maximal future evolution of small perturbations: stationary or periodic endstates, angular cascades, nonconvergence or curvature singularity, with rigorous basins.
