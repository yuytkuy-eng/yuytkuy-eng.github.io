Problem

Definitions

Use a time-oriented four-dimensional Lorentzian spacetime of signature (−,+,+,+)(-,+,+,+)(−,+,+,+) satisfying G(g)+Λg=8πTG(g)+\Lambda g=8\pi TG(g)+Λg=8πT. Initial data (Σ,h,K)(\Sigma,h,K)(Σ,h,K) obey R(h)+(tr⁡K)2−∣K∣2=16πμ+2ΛR(h)+(\operatorname{tr}K)^2-|K|^2=16\pi\mu+2\LambdaR(h)+(trK)2−∣K∣2=16πμ+2Λ and div⁡(K−(tr⁡K)h)=8πJ\operatorname{div}(K-(\operatorname{tr}K)h)=8\pi Jdiv(K−(trK)h)=8πJ. The MGHD is the maximal globally hyperbolic development.

Crushing means uniform mean-curvature divergence along compact Cauchy slices; CMC time is τ=tr⁡K\tau=\operatorname{tr}Kτ=trK and its lapse solves an elliptic equation.

Assumptions

Cosmological vacuum developments with compact Cauchy surfaces; specify initial conditions for singularity formation.

Claim

Prove long-time existence of the CMC gauge and control its approach to a crushing singularity, or classify failure.
