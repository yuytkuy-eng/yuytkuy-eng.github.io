Problem

Definitions

Let H_L=Σ_{j=1}^L h_{j,j+1} be a periodic, translation-invariant, frustration-free spin chain with fixed finite local dimension, algebraic positive-semidefinite h, and ||h||=1. Assume that the infinite chain has a unique injective-MPS ground state and periodic thermodynamic gap Δ>0. Let γ_n be the optimal certified lower bound produced at locality n by the local translation-invariant SDP hierarchy of Rai et al.

Assumptions

Translation-invariant periodic one-dimensional fixed-site dimension, unique injective MPS ground state and positive bulk gap; the specified SDP hierarchy.

Claim

Prove that

limn→∞γn=Δ,lim_{n→∞} γ_n = Δ,limn→∞​γn​=Δ,

or give one explicit chain satisfying the hypotheses for which

supnγn<Δ.sup_n γ_n < Δ.supn​γn​<Δ.
