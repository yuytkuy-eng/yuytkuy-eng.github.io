The triangular Abrikosov lattice without a periodic ansatz

UNSOLVED
Fields
Superconductivity
Topics
Ginzburg-LandauvorticesAbrikosov latticecrystallization

Problem

Assumptions

Two-dimensional global minimizers in the specified joint volume and parameter limit approaching Hc2H_{c2}Hc2​ from below, without a periodicity assumption.

Claim

In the two-dimensional Ginzburg–Landau model, in the thermodynamic regime with applied field just below the second critical field H_c2, prove that global energy minimizers have asymptotically triangular vortex order, without assuming periodicity. A precise acceptable conclusion is convergence of the rescaled vortex process to the triangular-lattice minimizer of the limiting renormalized energy.
