Computing Jordan–Kronecker invariants of Lie algebras

Problem

Definitions

For a finite-dimensional Lie algebra g\mathfrak gg, put Ax(ξ,η)=⟨x,[ξ,η]⟩A_x(\xi,\eta)=\langle x,[\xi,\eta]\rangleAx​(ξ,η)=⟨x,[ξ,η]⟩. The Jordan–Kronecker invariants of the generic pencil Ax+λAaA_x+\lambda A_aAx​+λAa​ record the even-dimensional Jordan blocks grouped by eigenvalue and the odd-dimensional Kronecker blocks of sizes 2ki−12k_i-12ki​−1.

Assumptions

Semidirect products, Borel and parabolic algebras, centralizers of singular elements and low-dimensional classes.

Claim

Find uniform computation rules and prove the genericity, spectral multiplicities and Kronecker indices they produce.
