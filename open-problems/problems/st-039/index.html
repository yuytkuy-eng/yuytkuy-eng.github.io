The exact quantum value of the I3322 Bell inequality

CLAIMED
Fields
Quantum nonlocality
Topics
Bell inequalityI3322semidefinite programmingdimension witnesses

Problem

Definitions

For three binary measurements A_1,A_2,A_3 and B_1,B_2,B_3, define

I3322 = P(A1B1)+P(A1B2)+P(A1B3) +P(A2B1)+P(A2B2)+P(A3B1) -P(A2B3)-P(A3B2) -2P(A1)-P(A2)-P(B1),

where P(AiBj) is the probability that both designated outcomes occur. Local models satisfy I3322≤0.

Assumptions

Finite-dimensional tensor strategies with unbounded local dimension; distinguish spatial closure and commuting strategies.

Claim

Determine the exact supremum of I3322 over all bipartite quantum states and measurements, and decide whether it is attained in any finite local dimension.
